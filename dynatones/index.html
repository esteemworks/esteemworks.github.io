DynaTones

Your desktop follows the sun.

A free app for Windows 11. It changes the wallpaper through the day and switches the color mode at sunrise and sunset, from one calculation of the sun.

Download Source on GitHub

Version 0.4.0, 57 MB. Free, source code on GitHub.

What it does

Light that flows

Before the next image, DynaTones blends the two a little more every minute, over 10, 30 or 60 minutes. The same place changes its light slowly.

Right in June and in December

Each image has its place on the sun's arc, rising or setting. A noon image shows at noon in winter too.

Wallpaper and color mode together

Color mode by the sun with offsets, at fixed times or following the image. Apps, Windows and the mouse pointer each on their own.

Your own images

Drop a folder or a .zip on the window. Auto places the images from the time and place in each photo, or from how bright they look.

Stays on track

After sleep, unlock, a changed clock or a new monitor, DynaTones checks what should show and puts it back.

The lock screen too

If you want, the lock screen follows the day as well, with the desktop's images or a package of its own.

Questions

Does DynaTones need my location?

No. It uses Windows location when it is on, else a place or coordinates you enter, else the time zone. Settings, Location, shows which one is in use and why.

Does it change the sign-in screen?

No. It changes your lock screen if you ask it to. The screen before signing in needs a policy of the device.

Can I use dynamic wallpapers I already have?

Yes. A folder of images, a .zip or a .ddw theme becomes a package, by button or by dropping it on the window.

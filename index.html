The apps

Two apps so far, more to come

StartQueue

Startup manager

Your apps after sign-in, in your order, ready when you sit down.

  • One list for every app that starts with Windows
  • Profiles by weekday and hours, quiet starts in the background
  • Shut down with your apps prepared for the next sign-in
  • Shows when each app started at the last sign-in, and which failed

DynaTones

Wallpaper and color mode by the sun

New

Your desktop follows the sun.

  • The wallpaper and the color mode from one calculation of the sun
  • Light that flows: before the next image, the two blend a little more every minute
  • Any folder of your own photos becomes a package
  • The lock screen can follow the day as well

For organizations

Managed with the tools you already use

DynaTones can be rolled out per device and managed centrally. StartQueue follows.

  • Group Policy and IntuneADMX templates in English and German.
  • Fixed or a defaultA fixed setting is greyed out with "Set by your organization". A default is a start users may change.
  • Install per deviceSilent setup for all users. Updates stay in the hands of your IT.
  • Docs for adminsEvery policy with its registry value, event ids and the command line, in the app's guide for admins.

Free apps for Windows 11.

Their source code is open under the MIT license; the shared library inside them is free to use with the apps, but not open source.

No account and no telemetry: the apps connect only to GitHub, for updates and the gallery of DynaTones, and this site sets no cookies.

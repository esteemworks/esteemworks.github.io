StartQueue

Your apps after sign-in, in your order, ready when you sit down.

A free startup manager for Windows 11: one startup order for every autostart app, with profiles, quiet starts and prepared shutdowns.

What it does

One list for everything that starts

Every app that starts with Windows, wherever it was set up, in one order. Drag to sort, add a delay, or wait until an app really runs.

Starts early, shows how long it took

Your first apps start right after sign-in, and StartQueue shows when each one started. On the author's PC the first app starts about 8 seconds after sign-in instead of about 60.

Ready when you sit down

Shut down with your apps prepared: at the next start Windows signs you in and locks the screen, and your apps start behind the lock screen.

Profiles for work and weekend

Weekdays, hours or all day, in an order you drag. Plan a pause like an out-of-office reply. Each profile has its own color.

Quiet starts

Normal, minimized or straight to the notification area, per app. StartQueue knows the start parameters of many apps and learns new ones.

The autostart stays clean

When an app adds itself again, StartQueue notices at once and takes it over, switches it off or only tells you, as you choose.

Download

For Windows 11, and it runs on Windows 10 with a few details missing. Without admin rights, StartQueue manages your own autostart entries. The full feature set needs an installation with admin rights.

Questions

Will my PC start faster?

Windows itself does not boot faster. Your apps start earlier and in the order you choose, and the window shows when each one started at the last sign-in. The biggest step is a prepared shutdown: your apps start before you sit down.

Is a prepared shutdown safe?

Afterwards the PC is signed in but locked, as after Windows+L. Unlocking needs your PIN, password or Windows Hello as always. Messengers may show you as online before you sit down.

Can I undo everything?

Yes. Every entry can be given back to Windows on its own, and uninstalling gives back all of them.
